Clip Engine

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Information processed

Clip Engine processes campaign configuration, source links, clip metadata, publishing records, deadlines, and analytics snapshots locally on your computer.

TikTok data

If you authorize TikTok, Clip Engine receives the account identity, profile statistics, public video metadata, publish time, share URL, and video metrics made available by TikTok. This data is used only to display and track analytics in the local application.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored locally using the operating system credential protection available to the application. Clip Engine does not store TikTok passwords and does not sell personal information.

Sharing and retention

Data remains on your computer unless you choose to send it to a connected platform or service. You can disconnect an integration and delete local application data using your operating system’s application-data controls.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator who supplied this application.
